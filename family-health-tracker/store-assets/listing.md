## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Family Health Tracker is a private, on-device health tracker for one person or a whole household — with no account, no server, and no network calls, ever. Every profile keeps its own data, and you can check the privacy claim yourself: the app works identically in airplane mode.

**Track what matters, for everyone in your household**
Heart rate, sleep, meals, medications, weight, blood pressure, glucose, mood, symptoms, water intake, and steps. Create a profile for every family member — each with its own data, its own layout, and its own medication list.

**See the patterns**
Individual trend charts for every metric (day/week/month/custom range), plus a Family Comparison view that shows how household members' numbers move together side by side, with automatic correlation flagging.

**Lab results, organized**
Log thyroid, vitamin, lipid, metabolic, and other lab panels. Values are automatically flagged against reference ranges so out-of-range results stand out immediately.

**Healthy tips and optional reminders**
Browse a library of everyday wellness tips, and turn on optional daily-tip or medication reminders whenever you want — powered entirely by your device's own notification system, never a push service.

**Scan a nutrition label**
Photograph a food package's nutrition facts panel and the app reads the numbers on-device using on-board text recognition. Nothing is saved until you review and confirm it.

**Bring your history with you**
Import data from an Apple Health export or a plain CSV file to build a long-term picture instead of starting from zero.

**Medical documents, all in one place**
Upload lab results, imaging reports, or visit summaries (PDF or photo) and link specific readings from them straight into a profile's timeline and charts.

**Built for real households**
A per-profile customization engine lets everyone show, hide, and reorder the trackers and navigation items they actually use, and choose icon-only, text-only, or icon+text buttons throughout the app.

**Why on-device matters**
There is no backend. Data is stored using your device's own storage, is never uploaded anywhere, and is permanently deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its storage — because we never had a copy to begin with. Every optional feature that touches a camera or sends a notification says so clearly and asks first.

Whether you're tracking for yourself, keeping an eye on a parent's medications, or managing a household of kids' checkups, Family Health Tracker keeps it organized — and keeps it private.
