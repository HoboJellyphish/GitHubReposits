# Privacy Policy — Sleep Tracker

**Last updated:** August 3, 2026

## Summary

Sleep Tracker does not collect, transmit, store, or share any of your data on any server. Everything you log — sleep times, wake times, nap times, and medication entries — stays only on your own device.

## What data the app handles

The app stores the following information, entered directly by you:

- Sleep and wake timestamps
- Nap start/end timestamps
- Medication name, dosage, and notes you choose to log, with a timestamp

## Where this data is stored

All data is saved locally on your device using standard on-device storage (`AsyncStorage`). It is never sent to us or to any third party, because the app has no server, no account system, and makes no network requests of any kind.

## Data sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit your data to anyone, because the app never has access to it in the first place — it exists only on your device.

## Third-party services

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

## Data deletion

Since all data lives only on your device, you can delete it at any time by:

- Deleting individual entries within the app (History tab → tap an entry → Delete), or
- Uninstalling the app, which removes all locally stored data.

## Medical disclaimer

Sleep Tracker is a personal logging tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult a healthcare professional for medical concerns, including questions about medication or sleep health.

## Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, since it collects no data at all beyond what you choose to store locally on your own device.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, an updated version will be posted at the same location with a revised "Last updated" date.
